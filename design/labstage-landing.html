Questions doctors and labs ask.

Can I skip a stage?
Yes. You choose the stages in the order form. A monolithic crown can skip layering, and the tracker only shows the stages you picked.
What if the lab declines my order?
You are told straight away and nothing is charged. Send the same order to another lab in one tap.
Which files can I send?
Scans as STL or PLY, photos as JPG, and PDFs, straight into the case. The lab sends its design files back the same way.
How are labs ranked?
By the ratings doctors give after each case. Labs that pay to be featured appear first and are always marked Sponsored.
Does it work in Arabic?
Every screen works in English and Arabic, and the stage names are the ones labs already use.
What does a lab pay?
The first month is free, then EGP 2,000 a month. Labstage keeps 5% of each order from the lab's payout.
